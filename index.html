Good to know

The questions we get asked most, answered plainly.

How far ahead should we book an Asian wedding DJ?

Peak Saturdays in summer go twelve to eighteen months ahead. Because we only take one wedding a day, a date is either free or it isn't — there's no second team to fall back on. If your date is set, it's worth checking early even if nothing else is decided yet.

Do you cover the ceremony and the reception if they're in different places?

Yes. The Full day and Production packages include a second crew, so one team can be setting up the reception room while the other is still running the ceremony. Both crews are ours, briefed in the same planning meetings.

Can we choose the music, or does the DJ decide?

Both. You give us the must-plays, the do-not-plays and the family songs that matter, and we build the structure around them. The rest is read live on the night — a floor doesn't follow a spreadsheet.

Do you travel for destination weddings?

Regularly. We either fly out with a laptop and control kit to play on a trusted local rig, or ship the full production. Freight, local crew and power are handled by us, and we arrive on site two days before.

How much does a wedding DJ package cost?

Every wedding is quoted individually after a short call, because guest numbers, venue restrictions, the number of rooms and travel change the answer significantly. Send us your date and venue and we'll come back with a full breakdown the same day.

What happens if the DJ is ill on the day?

We carry a contracted standby DJ who sits in on your planning meetings and holds a copy of your full brief, plus a duplicate set of core kit. It has happened twice in fifteen years and neither couple noticed a change on the day.
