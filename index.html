My Story

Learning to
Love Yourself

Learning to Love Yourself is one of the most important lessons I have learned in life. It is a profound understanding of your inner self, embracing your unique qualities, and cultivating a positive attitude towards life.

As a woman with a simple heart, my devotion lies with my family, and I find immense joy in supporting my sons as they navigate their respective career paths.

"I proudly embrace my Armenian Russian heritage and express the sophisticated style and elegance of my culture in my work."

The World of Fashion is a very tough and competitive industry. You have to back yourself to push past the fear of rejection. I learned how to turn these obstacles into challenges and faced them head-on.

"I want to be a role model and inspire all women both young and old that your body is your temple."
